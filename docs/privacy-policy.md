# Tengen-Toppa Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 20 October 2026_

Tengen-Toppa is a personal job-application assistant operated by Kanishk Singh Parihar for his own use. It is not offered to the public.

## What data is processed
- WhatsApp messages sent to the Tengen-Toppa business number by its operator (commands, job links and answers to application questions), together with the sender's phone number and message IDs.
- Job postings and the operator's own resume and application details.

## How it is used
Messages are used only to run the operator's job search: to add jobs, tailor the operator's resume, fill job applications and send status updates back on WhatsApp. Messages from any other number are ignored and not stored.

## Storage and sharing
All data is stored locally on the operator's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Job details and resume text are processed by the operator's own AI assistant subscription to produce tailored documents.

## Retention and deletion
Data is kept only as long as the operator needs it for the job search. To request deletion of any data, email kanishksinghparihar64@gmail.com and it will be deleted within 30 days.
